# Credits — qz YouTube edition

This 45-second film uses actual qz extension interactions recorded in an isolated Chrome window. Live provider requests generated the reading summary, product comparison, saved table, image collection, and reusable site Skill. Recording excerpts play at their original speed; waiting time is removed with cuts. Browser tab selection and result browsing were operated during capture. Provider/model labels are masked in the final edit. No application screens or results are simulated.

## Music and editing

Original 120 BPM instrumental synthesized for this film. No third-party music samples. Beat-aligned cuts, fixed framing, antialiased corners, side-panel compositing, and model-label masking were applied to recorded browser footage. Recording excerpts play at 1× speed. The four-panel sequence uses a shared five-second wall-clock interval from four concurrently recorded task windows. The multilingual ending retains its original timing and motion, redrawn in Alibaba PuHuiTi 2. Added video typography also uses Alibaba PuHuiTi 2. Native browser and extension typography is preserved.

## Custom Skill UI

The final product shot shows the actual saved `wikipedia-city-photos` tool result expanded in Chat, with its manifest-defined summary cards and `image_grid` renderer. The recorded UI displays data returned by the previously verified live Skill invocation.

## Concurrent tasks and child research

The four-panel shot records four real qz tasks running simultaneously: NASA Webb reading, Apple laptop comparison, Hangzhou photo collection, and an HTML travel-guide build. The source seven-second recording interval is checked against each task's actual start and end times in `qa/concurrency-check.json`.

The child-agent sequence comes from a separate actual parent task. Two children researched West Lake walks and Longjing tea experiences concurrently, creating tables with 9 and 10 rows. The parent combined them into an 11-row itinerary. The footage shows the native task tree while both children run, followed by the actual combined artifacts. Browser inspection and result browsing during capture were operated locally. All seven new parent/child and independent tasks completed.

Additional source titles and links appear in the recorded research results, including NASA Science, TravelChinaGuide, China Discovery, Wikipedia, and Viator. The movie shows their incidental citations inside actual qz task output.

## YouTube edit

This 45-second edition reuses the original source recordings. Browser pages and native sidebars are cropped and framed separately at selected points to improve readability. The original gallery showcase is replaced by Wikipedia reading and scripting; the Skill result card remains as a three-second custom-UI example. The parallel research task and its combined artifacts receive separate close-ups. Source clips retain 1× playback speed; pauses are removed through cuts. The original instrumental is rearranged for the new 120 BPM cut grid and normalized to approximately −16 LUFS. The seven-second multilingual ending includes the GitHub address. The thumbnail uses crops from the same Hangzhou recording.
