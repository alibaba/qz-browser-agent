Credits — qz YouTube edition

This 45-second film uses actual qz extension interactions recorded in an isolated Chrome window. Concurrent tasks and child research

The four-panel shot records four real qz tasks running simultaneously: NASA Webb reading, Apple laptop comparison, Hangzhou photo collection, and an HTML travel-guide build. The source seven-second recording interval is checked against each task's actual start and end times in qa/concurrency-check.json.

The child-agent sequence comes from a separate actual parent task. Two children researched West Lake walks and Longjing tea experiences concurrently, creating tables with 9 and 10 rows. The parent combined them into an 11-row itinerary. The footage shows the native task tree while both children run, followed by the actual combined artifacts. Browser inspection and result browsing during capture were operated locally. All seven new parent/child and independent tasks completed.

Additional source titles and links appear in the recorded research results, including NASA Science, TravelChinaGuide, China Discovery, Wikipedia, and Viator. The movie shows their incidental citations inside actual qz task output.
